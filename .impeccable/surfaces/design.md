# Surface brief: design (all screens of Silent Whisker)

Scope: every screen of the app as high-fidelity HTML mocks in `design/mocks/`; shape briefs per
area in `design/briefs/`. Visitor mode: Operate for the app, Persuade for the landing page, Read
for the legal pages. Audience: Hebrew-speaking volunteers outdoors, one-handed, in glare.
Constraints: Hebrew right-to-left first, 360 px to desktop, every string by key, no inline styles,
logical CSS properties. Unresolved decisions: `design/briefs/10-open-questions.md`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: content lives on a white wall, controls live on a floating pane of slate-blue glass.
Refuses the cream-and-terracotta pet-app look, the neon-on-dark map look and the gamified
volunteer look.

OWN-WORLD: pure white ground, one slate-blue for the shell, buttons and selection, five state
colours that always carry an icon and a word (green, orange, red, grey, plum for cat alerts), the
phone's system sans, flat surfaces with 1 px lines, one soft lift and a tinted blurred glass for
the navigation and form action bars.

STORY: a stranger understands in seconds that a red pin needs food, that a log counts only when
the phone is at the spot, and that nothing here shames a volunteer; then does one thing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the map full-bleed; a floating glass bar with four tabs; a pin card floating above
it with the spot name, two state chips and one button; a round locate control and a round "+".

FORM: candidate 6 of 7 on my grounded list (the apartment stairwell), seed key e3601352; the owner
chose this look ("slate blue on white") from five rendered looks and asked for the liquid-glass nav.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
