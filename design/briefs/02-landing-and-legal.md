# 2. Landing, privacy policy, terms

Mode: Persuade for the landing page (the only public page with ads), Read for the two legal pages.

## Job and audience
A signed-out visitor (shared link, search, a friend's phone) decides in seconds whether this is
for them and, if so, signs in. A cautious one (a person who feeds cats in a private yard) wants to
know where their location goes. Google's reviewers and ad system need the privacy policy and terms
reachable from the landing page.

## Outcome and proof
- First viewport: what it is in one sentence, the one thing that is true only here ("a log counts
  only when the phone is at the spot"), the **Sign in with Google** button, a language switch.
- Then three steps (find a spot in need, feed, log at the spot) with the real marker designs, not
  stock art; a short privacy promise (exact positions, custom spots shown only to members who are
  close or when the spot needs help, phone location not stored); "free and open source"; donate
  link; one reserved ad slot; footer with privacy, terms, source code and the contact address (D-74). The
  terms are accepted on the sign-up form, not here (D-69), so there is no "by continuing" line.
- No numbers about users, spots or cats (none exist yet; claims are never invented).
- On a phone the first screen is the pitch alone, on a slate gradient band; the phone frame starts below
  it and hangs over the band's edge. The three steps are numbered. A tinted closing section stands apart
  from a slate footer (mark, promise, links, "Made with a heart by Bar Almog").

## Selected direction
Inherit the wall and band; the landing is where the band is bigger: the lower part of the first
viewport is the dado with the sign-in button on it. The top of the page is the wall with the
logo. The page is a real, quiet page, not a hero template.

## States and ranges
Hebrew, English, Arabic and Russian (D-85), 360 px to desktop. EU visitor consent banner (Q10). Ad slot with reserved
height so the page never jumps. Legal pages are long reading text, 65 characters wide at most.

## Interaction and layout
Landing: one column on phone; from 1024 px two columns (copy and sign-in at inline-start, the
three marker steps at inline-end). Legal pages: a sticky "contents" list on desktop, a plain
heading list on phone.

## Boundaries
Ads only here. No map and no spot data for signed-out people. The legal texts are written by the
owner; the mocks carry a **draft** marked as such (a lawyer reads it before launch, D-75). The privacy policy lists what is kept, the
  location uses, who sees names, where the data is stored and who else receives it, the member's rights and
  how to use them, and the contact address.

## Open decisions
Q10 (ads and the EU consent banner), Q11 (donation link home).
