# Direction: the stairwell wall

How the look was chosen (Impeccable `shape` → new-work, Operate mode). The result is in
[DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md); this file keeps the reasoning.

## Scene (forces light, not dark)

A volunteer crouches by a feeding station at dawn or dusk, in a courtyard or beside a stairwell,
phone in one hand and a bowl in the other, thumb only. Light swings from low sun glare to a single
streetlamp. The screen must read in glare first, so it is light with high contrast; night use is
served by contrast, not by a second theme (open question Q1).

## Seven grounded candidates (cultural home: Israeli streets and buildings)

Ordered by resonance with the audience. The Impeccable roll assigned number 6.

1. Survey of Israel / hiking-map sheet: sand ground, contour tints, trail colours.
2. Urban street-name plates: white lettering on blue enamel, Hebrew over Latin.
3. Israel National Trail blazes: stripes of paint on rock.
4. Tel Aviv White City: rendered walls, shuttered balconies, ribbon windows.
5. Encaustic cement tiles of old entrance halls.
6. **The apartment stairwell: a painted dado band below, white render above, a thin line between
   them.** (assigned, built)
7. Makolet hand-written shelf cards on bright stock.

Families covered: cartography, signage, painted waymarks, architecture, tile, domestic paint,
print vernacular.

## Why number 6 carries the product

Every Israeli volunteer knows the stairwell: oil-painted lower wall (sage, blue, grey), white above,
a hand-drawn line between. It is the cats' own territory, it is quiet, and it is a ready-made rule
for a shell: **content on the white wall, controls on the painted band.** The world lends only
what Operate allows: palette (white render, one deep slate-blue enamel, five state colours), type
(plain system sans), density (generous, 48 px targets) and one signature move (the chair-rail line
between band and wall). Navigation and controls stay standard.

## Challengers weighed (the roll dealt six; verdicts)

- Warm consumer app (soft cards, one action colour): **competitive** (clear, thumb-reachable,
  but generic, and its cream ground is the default the design must avoid). Raise taken: the accent
  colour is only ever on something that can be tapped.
- Boarding pass and gate board: **declined**. Kept: tabular figures and fixed columns for the
  "last log 14 h ago" timers.
- Timetable slide rack: **declined**. Kept: state is a mark in a fixed cell, never colour alone
  (marker corner badges, chips with an icon and a word).
- Type specimen, catalog sleeve, acetate manual: **declined** (no product fit, costume risk).

## Standing exit

The category standard (a white Material-style map app with a blue FAB) was not chosen; it would
need no further reasoning if the owner prefers it. The owner approves or re-rolls at review.

## Direction contract

Also stored with `impeccable surface-brief` at `.impeccable/surfaces/design.md`.

- **THESIS:** content lives on a white wall, controls live on a painted band, and one thin line
  divides them. Refuses the cream-and-terracotta pet-app look, the neon-on-dark map look and
  the gamified volunteer look.
- **OWN-WORLD:** white render ground, slate-blue enamel band, five state colours with an icon each
  (green, orange, red, grey, plum for cat alerts), system sans, flat surfaces with 1 px lines.
- **STORY:** a stranger understands in seconds that a red pin needs food, that a log counts only at
  the spot, and that nothing here shames a volunteer; then does one thing.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** the map full-bleed, band nav at the bottom (side rail from 768 px), a pin
  sheet that peeks above the band, a round "+" and a locate button above it.
- **FORM:** candidate 6 of 7, seed key e3601352.
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review,
  the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
