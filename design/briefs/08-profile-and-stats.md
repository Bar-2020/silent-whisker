# 8. Profile, stats, settings, admin lists

Mode: Operate.

## Job and audience
A member checks their stats, edits their name, city or photo choice, turns location back on, and
switches language. An admin reaches the waiting list from here.

## Outcome and proof
- Header: avatar (Google photo if chosen, initials otherwise), name, city; the edit action is a pencil
  in the top bar.
- **Stats** ("My activity"): cats helped, feedings, water given, as three plain rows on a tinted card with
  no dividers. Stats carry no prize: no badges, levels or streaks. (Q24: what "cats helped" counts.)
- **Settings**: one list: language (Hebrew, English), location permission status with the steps to allow it
  permanently in each browser, **install as an app** (a guide per browser, Q28), sign out, delete
  account (Q25). The Google photo choice lives in Edit, not here.
- **Support**: donate link, source code, privacy, terms.
- **Admin** (shown only to admins): "waiting for approval" with a count, and "reported content"
  (Q6).
- **Edit** (name, city, photo choice): the same fields and the same errors as first run.

## Selected direction
A settings list in the system's plain rows: icon, title, value, chevron. No hero, no big stat
numbers: the numbers are tabular and the same size as the labels.

## States and ranges
Photo chosen, not chosen, none from Google; location granted, denied, not asked; saving; city not
found; text refused; empty admin list; banned member (a persistent notice that writes are off,
Q29). Name up to 60 characters.

## Interaction and layout
Phone: one scroll with grouped rows. Desktop: two columns, identity and stats at the inline-start,
settings at the inline-end.

## Boundaries
Changing the picture itself is v2. No public profile for other members.

## Open decisions
Q6, Q24, Q25, Q26, Q29.
