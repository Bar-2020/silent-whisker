# 7. Chat (per spot)

Mode: Operate.

## Job and audience
Volunteers who feed the same spot coordinate in short messages ("fed at 7, bowl was empty",
"a new cat showed up"). Not a social network: small, per spot, moderated automatically.

## Outcome and proof
A plain thread opened from the spot page: messages oldest to newest with the composer at the
bottom, "earlier messages" at the top (Q23). Each message: initials or Google photo, name (first
name and last initial, Q12), time, text. Tapping a message opens a dialog: copy, report; for admins also
restrict chat and ban the author. Admins have a three-dots menu at the end of the top bar (Q39).

## Selected direction
No bubbles with tails and no colours per person; own messages sit at the inline-end with a tinted
background, others at the inline-start on the plain wall. Text direction follows the text itself
(Hebrew and English mixed in one thread).

## States and ranges
Empty, loading, 1 to 100 messages per page, sending (the text is checked before it is accepted),
in review (saved while the daily cost breaker holds checks back, visible only to the author),
refused by the text check (the message stays in the box with the reason), chat restricted
(composer replaced by a note and the end date), daily limit of 100 messages reached, rate limit,
banned. Message length 1 to 500 characters.

## Interaction and layout
Phone: full screen with back to the spot, the brand mark in the top bar; the composer is pinned to
the bottom in every state (a restriction, a limit or a ban replaces it with a notice). A refusal is a toast. Desktop: the
chat is a centred column beside the rail, not a modal.

## Boundaries
No images, reactions, read receipts, typing indicators or live updates (not in v1). Refresh is a
pull or a button.
