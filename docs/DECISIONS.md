| D-19 | "This spot was empty" button, for food or for water: makes that type red until the next counted log, marks the latest counted log of that type disputed, and does not penalise the logger by itself (an empty bowl usually means the cats ate). The reporter must be within the proof radius, and a report never disputes the reporter's own log (R-16). | accepted |
| D-21 | Profile picture: the member's Google account photo. Google's `picture` claim arrives through Cognito's attribute mapping at each sign-in; our server alone copies it into `avatar_url`, only if it is a Google image address, and members cannot write `avatar_url`. We keep only the URL and never copy the image into our storage. The picture is shown only if the member chose to show it (D-27); otherwise, and when Google sends none (the claim is not guaranteed), a default avatar with the member's initials is shown. No upload and no admin review in v1: the member opts in to showing a photo from their own Google account, which Google moderates, so the hard rules stay unchanged. Changing the picture inside the app is v2, with a limit and moderation. | accepted |
| D-22 | Money guardrails: the app stays free for the first 6 months on the AWS Free plan credit ($100 at sign-up plus up to $100 for activities). The plan ends after 6 months or when the credit is used, then the account closes unless upgraded to Paid (AWS keeps the data 90 days). A daily moderation cap of about $0.50 (R-05; at most $15 a month, about $91 over the 6 free months), an AWS Budget of $20 a month with email alerts, and an upgrade to the Paid plan before the plan ends. | accepted |
| D-23 | When a limit is hit, refuse or save? R-09, R-10 and R-13 say the log is saved as unverified, and R-05 says uploads wait as pending, but [API.md](API.md) refuses with `TOO_SOON` and `CAP_REACHED`. Recommendation: save as unverified or pending, so nothing an honest member did is lost, and change the API refusals. | open |
| D-25 | The phone must report a location accuracy of 50 m or better for a log to count, so a vague reading cannot pass the proof radius check (R-09). The limit is a constant. | assumed |
| D-26 | DynamoDB capacity mode (Phase 1): the always-free allowance (25 GB, 25 write and 25 read units per Region and account) is for provisioned capacity only; on-demand tables are billed (Tel Aviv: $0.85 per million writes, $0.17 per million reads, under $1 a month at 1,000 members). Recommendation: provisioned, splitting the 25 units across the tables, and on-demand only for a table that outgrows its share. | open |
| D-27 | The app asks three things at the first sign-in: the member's full name, whether to show their Google photo, and their city. The map opens centred on that city. | accepted |
